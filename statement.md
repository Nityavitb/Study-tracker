# Project Statement

## Project Title

**Study Hours Tracker**

---

## Problem Statement

Students often study for different amounts of time across different subjects and may find it difficult to keep track of their study hours, study goals, and upcoming tasks in one place.

The Study Hours Tracker is designed to provide a simple way for students to record their study information, set a study goal, maintain a checklist, and view their study progress.

---

## Scope of the Project

The scope of the Study Hours Tracker includes:

* Recording the student's name and study-session information.
* Recording the number of hours studied.
* Setting a study goal in hours.
* Recording the subject and topic studied.
* Maintaining a general to-do list.
* Maintaining subject-wise study checklists.
* Recording the number of completed study sessions.
* Calculating study-goal progress.
* Determining whether the study goal has been achieved.
* Displaying a summary of the study session.

The current project focuses on a Python-based command-line application.

---

## Target Users

The primary target users are:

* School students
* College students
* Students preparing for examinations
* Students who want to monitor their study time and goals

---

## High-Level Features

### 1. Study Session Tracking

The application collects information about the student's study session, including name, subject, topic, study hours, and study goal.

### 2. Goal Tracking

The application compares the student's study hours with the study goal and calculates the percentage of the goal completed.

### 3. To-Do Checklist

The application allows students to enter a general to-do list and subject-wise tasks for upcoming study sessions.

### 4. Study Report

The application generates a summary containing the student's study information, number of completed sessions, goal progress, and checklist.

### 5. Modular Implementation

The project separates major functionality into different Python modules to make the application easier to organize and maintain.

---

## Project Objective

The objective of the Study Hours Tracker is to provide students with a simple command-line tool for recording study activities and monitoring progress toward their study goals while applying Python programming concepts in a practical project.
